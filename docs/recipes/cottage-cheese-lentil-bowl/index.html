Cottage Cheese & Lentil Power Bowl

Ingredients

  • 200 g cottage cheese
  • 1 tin (240 g) green lentils, drained
  • 100 g cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 cucumber, diced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Handful of mint

Method

  1. Toss the lentils, tomatoes and cucumber with the oil and lemon juice.
  2. Spoon over the cottage cheese and scatter with mint.
