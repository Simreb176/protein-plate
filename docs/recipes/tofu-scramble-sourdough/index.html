Tofu Scramble on Sourdough

Ingredients

  • 180 g firm tofu, crumbled
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 slices sourdough
  • Handful of spinach

Method

  1. Heat the oil and soften the spinach for a minute.
  2. Add the crumbled tofu, turmeric and paprika and cook for 5 minutes, stirring.
  3. Season well and pile onto toasted sourdough.
