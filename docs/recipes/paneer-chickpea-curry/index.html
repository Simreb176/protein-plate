Paneer & Chickpea Curry

Ingredients

  • 150 g paneer, cubed
  • 1 tin (240 g) chickpeas, drained
  • 1 tin chopped tomatoes
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp curry powder
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 150 g cooked rice

Method

  1. Soften the onion in the oil, add the curry powder and cook for a minute.
  2. Add the tomatoes and chickpeas and simmer for 20 minutes.
  3. Stir in the paneer and serve with the rice.
