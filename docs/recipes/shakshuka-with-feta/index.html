Shakshuka with Feta

Ingredients

  • 1 tin chopped tomatoes
  • 1/2 onion, sliced
  • 1 red pepper, sliced
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 3 eggs
  • 40 g feta
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Method

  1. Soften the onion and pepper in the oil for 8 minutes.
  2. Add the cumin and tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes.
  3. Make three wells, crack in the eggs, scatter over the feta and cover until the whites are set.
