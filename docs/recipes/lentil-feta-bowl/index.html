Lentil & Feta Bowl

Ingredients

  • 1 tin (240 g) green lentils, drained
  • 60 g feta, crumbled
  • 100 g roasted beetroot
  • 2 handfuls of rocket
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard

Method

  1. Whisk the oil and mustard with a splash of water.
  2. Toss the lentils and beetroot in the dressing.
  3. Top with the feta and rocket.
