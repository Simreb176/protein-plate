Chicken, Quinoa & Roasted Veg Bowl

Ingredients

  • 150 g chicken breast
  • 60 g quinoa (dry)
  • 1 courgette, chopped
  • 1 red pepper, chopped
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice

Method

  1. Roast the courgette and pepper in the oil at 200°C for 20 minutes.
  2. Simmer the quinoa for 15 minutes, then drain.
  3. Griddle the chicken, slice it, and toss everything with the lemon juice.
