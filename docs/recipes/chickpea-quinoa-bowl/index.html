Roasted Chickpea & Quinoa Bowl

Ingredients

  • 1 tin (240 g) chickpeas, drained
  • 60 g quinoa (dry)
  • 1 red pepper, chopped
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 handfuls of rocket
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice

Method

  1. Toss the chickpeas and pepper with the oil and cumin and roast at 200°C for 20 minutes.
  2. Simmer the quinoa for 15 minutes and drain.
  3. Toss everything with the rocket and lemon juice.
