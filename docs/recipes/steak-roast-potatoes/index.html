Steak & Roast Potatoes

Ingredients

  • 200 g rump steak
  • 250 g potatoes, halved
  • 200 g green beans
  • 20 g butter
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 clove garlic

Method

  1. Roast the potatoes in the oil at 200°C for 30 minutes.
  2. Sear the steak for 3 minutes a side and rest for 5 minutes.
  3. Steam the beans, toss with the garlic butter, and serve everything together.
