Egg White & Feta Omelette

Ingredients

  • 5 egg whites
  • 1 whole egg
  • 40 g feta, crumbled
  • 100 g spinach
  • 1 tsp olive oil

Method

  1. Wilt the spinach in the oil, then pour over the beaten eggs.
  2. Cook gently until nearly set, scatter over the feta and fold.
