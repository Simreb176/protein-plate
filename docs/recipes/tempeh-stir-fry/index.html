Tempeh & Broccoli Stir-Fry

Ingredients

  • 180 g tempeh, sliced
  • 200 g broccoli
  • 150 g cooked brown rice
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 clove garlic, sliced

Method

  1. Fry the tempeh in the sesame oil until golden.
  2. Add the broccoli and garlic and stir-fry for 4 minutes.
  3. Add the soy sauce, warm the rice through and serve.
