Teriyaki Salmon Rice Bowl

Ingredients

  • 150 g salmon fillet
  • 150 g cooked jasmine rice
  • 100 g pak choi
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds

Method

  1. Mix the soy, honey and sesame oil.
  2. Pan-fry the salmon for 4 minutes a side, spooning over the sauce at the end.
  3. Wilt the pak choi, serve over the rice with the salmon, and scatter with sesame seeds.
