Black Bean & Sweet Potato Chilli

Ingredients

  • 1 large sweet potato, diced
  • 1 tin black beans, drained
  • 1 tin chopped tomatoes
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 150 g cooked rice

Method

  1. Soften the onion in the oil, then add the spices.
  2. Add the sweet potato, beans and tomatoes with a splash of water and simmer for 25 minutes.
  3. Serve with the rice.
