Turkey & Avocado Wrap

Ingredients

  • 120 g cooked turkey breast, sliced
  • 1 wholemeal wrap
  • 1/2 avocado
  • Handful of spinach
  • 1 tbsp Greek yoghurt
  • Squeeze of lime

Method

  1. Mash the avocado with the yoghurt and lime.
  2. Spread over the wrap, then layer in the turkey and spinach.
  3. Roll tightly and slice in half.
