Tuna & Oatcakes

Ingredients

  • 1 small tin (80 g) tuna in spring water
  • 3 oatcakes
  • 1 tsp light mayonnaise
  • Squeeze of lemon

Method

  1. Mix the tuna with the mayonnaise and lemon.
  2. Pile onto the oatcakes.
