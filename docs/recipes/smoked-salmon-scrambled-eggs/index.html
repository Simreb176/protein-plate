Smoked Salmon Scrambled Eggs

Ingredients

  • 3 eggs
  • 60 g smoked salmon
  • 20 g butter
  • 1 tbsp crème fraîche
  • 1 slice rye bread (optional)

Method

  1. Beat the eggs with a pinch of salt and pepper.
  2. Melt the butter in a pan over a low heat, add the eggs and stir slowly until just set.
  3. Fold through the crème fraîche and smoked salmon off the heat. Serve with the rye toast if you like.
